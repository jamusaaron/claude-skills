# Interview / expert protocol

Use when public sources are thin. This is not covert collection.

## Instrument (30–45 min)

1. Walk me through how you actually measure ______ .
2. What would a serious person on the other side say we are getting wrong?
3. Where is the best *primary* document or dataset, including ones that hurt your view?
4. What changed in the last 18 months that the literature has not caught?
5. If this finding were later overturned, what was the most likely miss?

Do **not** lead with your current synthesis. Do not treat one expert as corroboration of themselves.

## After

- Promote quotes to evidence cards with `source_type: interview`, date, consent flag.
- Independently verify any statistic they cited.
- Log refusals and no-contacts so coverage analysis sees the hole.
