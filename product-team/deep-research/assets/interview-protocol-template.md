# Interview Protocol (human sources)

**Research question this interview serves:**  
**Interviewee role (not a biography):**  
**Date / mode:**  
**Consent:** purpose explained; recording Y/N; attribution on/off record  
**Retention:** working notes vs deliverable (minimize PII in the latter)

## Map to desk-research questions

| Protocol Q | Research question id | Why a human is needed (not just a PDF) |
|------------|----------------------|----------------------------------------|
| | | |

## Opening

1. Purpose in one sentence
2. How this will be used
3. Permission to quote / paraphrase

## Core (behavior and evidence, not opinions first)

1. Walk me through the last time [event]. What happened, in order?
2. What artifact or data would corroborate that?
3. Where does the public story get this wrong?
4. What would falsify your current view?

## Disconfirming prompts

- What is the strongest case against your position?
- Who disagrees inside your institution, and what do they cite?
- If we only had [metric], what would we miss?

## Close

- Who else should we speak to (and who would they tell us to ignore)?
- Documents we may access lawfully?

## After

- Code the notes to claim ids the same day
- Send a factual accuracy check if on-record
- Do not let a single interview outvote a High primary on prevalence
